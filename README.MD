# 🌱 EcoSense AI — Real-Time Environmental Intelligence

EcoSense AI is an interactive sustainability dashboard that converts real-time air quality data into clear, understandable insights and practical, actionable recommendations. Built for non-technical users, urban citizens, students, and educators, the platform bridges the gap between environmental monitoring and meaningful everyday action.

## ✨ Key Features
- **🌫️ Live Air Quality Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of AQI, PM2.5, PM10, and key pollutants.
- **🗺️ Interactive Map & City Comparisons:** Visualize and compare environmental metrics across locations.
- **🤖 AI Sustainability Assistant:** Ask questions and receive context-aware recommendations for energy conservation, waste reduction, and sustainable living.
- **📊 Sustainability Score & Downloadable Reports:** Summarize environmental metrics into simple, accessible scores and exportable reports.
- **🛡️ Responsible AI Principles:** Built with transparency, privacy, fairness, and clear limitation boundaries.
